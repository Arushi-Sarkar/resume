My Journey

I'm pursuing a Diploma in Applied Artificial Intelligence at Temasek Polytechnic, where I've built a hands-on foundation in machine learning, data analytics, and cloud-based AI solutions. Alongside my studies, I'm currently working as a Data Engineer Intern at OCBC Bank, where I build automation pipelines and apply machine learning to fraud detection and sentiment analysis on real financial and news datasets.

My passion lies in creating technology with real-world impact — from an AI-driven skincare platform for teenagers to a RAG-powered medical diagnosis chatbot and a sustainability tracking app aligned with Singapore's Green Plan 2030. I like projects that force me to combine disciplines: machine learning with cloud infrastructure, automation with thoughtful UX, ethics with engineering.

Beyond the technical side, I've taken on leadership roles throughout my education — from Class Representative to Peer Support Leader — and I bring that same collaborative, communicative approach to every team I work with.
